# agencies.10 (v0.30.0-agencies.10)

Agency flags and colours across the game, plus a fixed flag picker.

- **Flag picker:** the agency flag selector now lists every installed flag (stock, mod and your own PNGs up to 1 MB), with search and pages. Your own flag uploads to the server automatically when you pick it.
- **Map and tracking station:** craft of agencies you can identify are tinted in their agency colour (orbit lines, icons, map captions, flight labels). The tracking station list shows the agency flag next to each craft.
- **Flight nameplates:** visible rival craft within range (default 2.5 km) show their agency flag and name above them.
- **Launch sites:** each assigned launch site shows its agency's flag in the KSC view, the tracking station and the map. Hidden Kerbal Konstructs sites stay hidden.
- **Auto craft flag:** craft launched with the default flag carry your agency flag. A flag you picked yourself is kept. Everyone who sees the craft sees the flag.
- **Chat and player list:** names show the player's agency flag and colour.
- **Agency window:** flags on leaderboard rows, firsts, trade offers, received designs and bought stock.
- **Designs tab:** shows the craft name instead of "Design 1a2b3c4d" (older tooling is backfilled from saved craft).
- Dark agency colours are lightened for text so names stay readable.
- Hidden or classified craft never get a tint, flag or nameplate.
- New Options section "Agency flags and colours" turns each part on or off.

Client and server must use the same build. No save format change from agencies.9.
